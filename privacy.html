Privacy

Your choices on this portfolio.

This notice describes the portfolio website’s analytics and contact form. The website owner is Mikhail; contact: ekkonwork@gmail.com.

Analytics are optional

Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity are not loaded until you choose “Allow analytics”. If you decline, the choice is saved in this browser and those services are not loaded. You can change the choice at any time using Privacy settings in the site footer.

After consent, analytics may record page views, selected work and series viewed, image/reference comparisons, contact-link clicks, copying the public email address and successful form submissions. Analytics uses an allowlist of event fields. Form text, name, email, product links and message contents are not sent as analytics event data. Advertising and ad-personalization features remain disabled.

Contact form

If you submit the contact form, the details you provide are sent to Mikhail through Formspree so that he can reply and discuss the project. The form asks for an email and project message; name, product/reference link, image count and timing are optional. Avoid sending confidential material. Formspree processes the submission under its own terms and privacy policy.

Cookies and storage

The site stores your analytics choice in this browser for up to six months and stores your selected language when available. Google Analytics and Clarity may set their own cookies after consent, according to their current policies. Declining or withdrawing consent stops further analytics loading; existing provider cookies are cleared where the browser allows.
